Status: Product planning specification  
Target site: https://omos.onegodian.com/

## Purpose

This document defines the first OMOS digital products for the shop pages. The goal is to convert the OMOS model portal into a revenue-producing documentation, prompt, training, and developer-kit storefront while keeping public claims legally disciplined and operationally accurate.

## Product Classification Rules

Use clear product categories:

- Digital download
- Developer kit
- Prompt pack
- Documentation pack
- Course / mini-course
- Membership resource
- Institutional explainer

Avoid presenting any digital product as a government document, securities instrument, legal substitute, or guaranteed technical runtime unless the backend and legal documentation support that claim.
