# Belief Mapper Production Plan

Status: Production preparation record
Owner: ONEGODIAN, LLC
Primary domain: OneGodian.org
Primary route target: `/belief-mapper`

## Purpose

The Belief Mapper is the public, mobile-first reflection entry point for the OneGodian digital experience. It should help visitors understand their relationship to belief, identity, unity, purpose, tradition, and community without coercion, conversion pressure, or institutional overstatement.

## Public Positioning Standard

Use this framing in product copy, documentation, and UI:

> The Belief Mapper is a voluntary identity reflection tool. It helps users explore what they already believe and where they may be on the OneGodian journey.

Do not present the Belief Mapper as:

- a conversion mechanism;
- a religious test;
- a governmental or civil authority process;
- a mandatory membership gate;
- a replacement for any existing faith tradition.

## Production MVP

The first production release should be Belief Mapper Lite.

Required MVP functions:

1. Mobile-first landing screen.
2. 3 to 5 reflection questions.
3. Tap or swipe optimized answer selection.
4. Instant result output.
5. Public-safe journey result language.
6. Shareable identity card copy.
7. Optional email capture only after the result is shown.
8. Links to recommended OneGodian.org content.
9. Privacy notice before any data retention.
10. No account requirement for first use.

## Recommended Public Journey Labels

Use public labels first, then map internally to canonical stages:

| Public Label | Internal Stage | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Explorer | Seeker | Exploring belief, meaning, and the One God concept. |
| Aligned | Believer | Affirms unity, purpose, and One Source/One Truth language. |
| Activated | OneGodian | Identifies with the OneGodian framework and wants deeper participation. |
| Guide | Elder | Demonstrates continuity, contribution, and community-facing wisdom. |

## Suggested Lite Questions

1. What do you believe connects all people?
2. Do you believe truth ultimately comes from one source?
3. What role does community play in your belief life?
4. How do you currently describe your spiritual identity?
5. Do you believe your life has a higher purpose?

## Result Screen Requirements

Every result page should include:

- a plain-language identity summary;
- a short reflection statement;
- a recommended next path;
- a shareable result phrase;
- links to OneGodian meaning, history, and introductory resources;
- a disclaimer that the result is reflective and voluntary.

## Privacy and Compliance Controls

Before production launch:

- disclose whether responses are stored;
- avoid collecting sensitive personal data unless necessary;
- make email submission optional;
- do not imply legal, religious, medical, psychological, or financial advice;
- avoid rank-based superiority language;
- preserve user dignity and voluntary participation.

## Definition of Done

The Belief Mapper is production-ready only when:

- `/belief-mapper` renders correctly on mobile and desktop;
- the user can complete the mapper in under 90 seconds;
- the result screen loads without account creation;
- all buttons have working destinations;
- analytics events are configured without exposing sensitive answers;
- privacy language is visible before any data capture;
- content uses public-safe OneGodian positioning;
- the page is linked from the relevant OneGodian.org navigation path;
- the deployment target is documented;
- the final version is reviewed before public promotion.

## Production Priority

This should be treated as a flagship onboarding system for OneGodian.org because it converts abstract identity explanation into interactive behavior. The first release should prioritize clarity, speed, safety, and shareability over feature complexity.
