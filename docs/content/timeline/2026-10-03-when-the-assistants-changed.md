## Milestone Summary

On October 3, 2026, OneGodian formally recorded a major continuity principle for its intelligence architecture:

> **Platforms change. Models change. Interfaces change. The record must survive them all.**

The immediate context was a period of rapid change in consumer AI-assistant platforms, including OpenAI's planned retirement of custom GPTs and migration toward plugins, alongside continued use of systems such as Gemini Gems and other vendor-specific assistants.

Rather than treating any external assistant as the permanent home of OneGodian intelligence, the architecture was reframed around **platform-independent preservation**.

## What Changed

The OneGodian architecture now treats the following as the durable source layer:

- canonical OneGodian instructions and operating rules;
- founder-defined identity and terminology records;
- the OneGodianese / OneGodianology™ lexicon;
- OMOS™ and OHI™ specifications;
- knowledge manifests and reference packages;
- authority-of-record files;
- version history and change records;
- evaluation and regression tests;
- structured skills and reusable workflows;
- tool, API, MCP, and connector contracts.

External AI systems may interpret, assist, execute, or provide interfaces to these materials, but they are not the authority of record.

## Architecture Principle

The milestone establishes this hierarchy:

**Canonical OneGodian Record → Versioned Knowledge / Rules → Skills → Tools & Actions → Vendor Adapters → AI Interfaces**

The vendor adapter is replaceable.  
The OneGodian record is not.

api.OneGodian.org remains the shared platform core for cross-property APIs, connectors, adapters, MCP, synchronization, events, and shared services, while specialized OneGodian properties remain authoritative for their own domains.

## Historical Significance

This milestone marks the transition from **assistant-centered preservation** to **institution-centered preservation**.

The important change was not simply moving from one AI product to another. It was recognizing that a body of work intended to survive across decades cannot depend on a single model, account type, interface, company, or product category.

The October 3 record therefore became part of the broader OneGodian institutional-continuity strategy:

**preserve the source → document the rules → version the knowledge → test the behavior → replace the interface when necessary.**
