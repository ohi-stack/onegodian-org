## SEO Sitemap Export Rules

The accompanying `sitemaps/onegodian-managed-public-candidates.xml` contains **22 proposed public URLs**. It excludes all 19 login-required routes, the public login/account page `/my-account/` (typically noindex), the Contributor Intake page (status not provided), and the two missing routes (already protected). The XML is a **candidate list**, **not** evidence that all 22 are published/indexable, and it is not a replacement for WordPress/SEO-plugin-generated `/wp-sitemap.xml` or `/sitemap_index.xml`.

Before publishing, resolve each URL on the live site and exclude redirects, errors, noncanonical pages, password-protected pages, and pages designated noindex by the active SEO system. Do not add fabricated `lastmod` dates. Existing unrelated public content must remain in the authoritative full-site SEO sitemap.
