  <div class="ogwhy-intro">
    <span class="ogwhy-label">MEANING / LANGUAGE / IDENTITY</span>
    <h2 id="ogwhy-definition-title">The meaning is in the word.</h2>
    <p>OneGodian was developed to name an identity in relationship to One God. Its definition has three connected parts.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="ogwhy-grid ogwhy-grid-three">
    <article class="ogwhy-card ogwhy-card-dark">
      <span class="ogwhy-index">01 / RELATIONSHIP</span>
      <h3>Related To</h3>
      <p>OneGodian begins with relationship to One God: the recognition of a divine source greater than the individual and the institutions people create.</p>
    </article>
    <article class="ogwhy-card ogwhy-card-dark ogwhy-card-purple">
      <span class="ogwhy-index">02 / REFLECTION</span>
      <h3>Resembling</h3>
      <p>Resembling One God does not mean becoming God. It means striving to reflect goodness through conscience, truth, compassion, justice, wisdom, creativity, and service.</p>
    </article>
    <article class="ogwhy-card ogwhy-card-dark ogwhy-card-teal">
      <span class="ogwhy-index">03 / BELONGING</span>
      <h3>Belonging To</h3>
      <p>Belonging expresses spiritual identity and responsibility toward God, ourselves, other people, future generations, and the world around us.</p>
    </article>
  </div>
  <p class="ogwhy-footnote">The definition expresses a religious and philosophical understanding; it does not automatically enroll anyone in an organization or determine another person’s beliefs.</p>
</section>

  <div class="ogwhy-intro">
    <span class="ogwhy-label">ORIGIN / THEOLOGY / LANGUAGE</span>
    <h2 id="ogwhy-origin-title">Why was the word created?</h2>
    <p>According to founder One Gregory OneGodian™, the search began with theology and language—not with a company, website, membership product, or technology.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="ogwhy-grid ogwhy-grid-two">
    <article class="ogwhy-card ogwhy-card-glass">
      <span class="ogwhy-index">THE SEARCH</span>
      <h3>Finding the right identity word</h3>
      <p>Gregory studied the idea of One God and considered different word forms, including “Onegodeism,” before arriving at “OneGodian” as the expression of relationship, resemblance, and belonging.</p>
      <p>The foundational question was direct: if people belong to One God, what identity word describes that relationship?</p>
    </article>
    <article class="ogwhy-card ogwhy-card-glass">
      <span class="ogwhy-index">SCRIPTURAL CONTEXT</span>
      <h3>One God as the theological center</h3>
      <p><strong>Ephesians 4:4–6</strong> speaks of one body, Spirit, hope, Lord, faith, baptism, and one God and Father of all. <strong>Mark 10:17–18</strong> directs attention to God as the ultimate source of goodness.</p>
      <p>These passages informed Gregory’s interpretation; OneGodian is his developed identity term, not a word quoted from either passage.</p>
    </article>
  </div>
  <div class="ogwhy-statement">
    <strong>The chronology matters.</strong>
    <p>The identity and original writings came before the websites, books, educational programs, memberships, businesses, registries, and technology platforms. The original authorship record and subsequent developments should be presented distinctly and supported by evidence.</p>
  </div>
